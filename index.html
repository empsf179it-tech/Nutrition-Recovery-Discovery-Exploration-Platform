Our Philosophy

The Science of NOURISH

Why we built the intersection of Cronometer's nutrient accuracy and Noom's behavioral recovery science.

For decades, athletes and wellness seekers have been forced to choose between boring calorie counters with tedious logging or generic recipe websites filled with vague wellness claims.

NOURISH was born to bridge the divide. We believe that physical recovery is not a passive waiting game—it is an active biochemical process. Every muscle micro-tear, depleted glycogen store, and disrupted sleep cycle is an opportunity for precise nutritional intervention.

The 3 Pillars of NOURISH

  • 1. Biological Timing (Chronobiology): Nutrients delivered at the exact moment of peak cellular receptivity produce 3x the restorative benefit.
  • 2. Anti-Inflammatory Downregulation: Modulating post-exercise inflammation without blunting positive athletic adaptations.
  • 3. Behavioral Micro-Routines: Seamless psychological habit cues inspired by Noom, turning recovery into an effortless lifelong ritual.

Frequently Asked Questions

How quickly will the AI Recovery Coach respond?

Our AI coach responds instantly. For clinical inquiries that require human review, our certified sports dietitians typically respond within 4 hours during business days.

Are the nutrition plans personalized?

Yes, all our plans are tailored to your specific biomarker data, training intensity, and recovery goals. The AI adapts your macros dynamically based on your daily inputs.

Can I integrate this with my fitness tracker?

Absolutely. NOURISH Lab syncs perfectly with Apple Health, Garmin, Oura, and Whoop to pull in sleep data, HRV, and daily energy expenditure for real-time recovery protocol adjustments.

What happens if I miss a scheduled recovery meal?

No problem at all! The AI will automatically recalculate your remaining macros for the day and suggest an optimized next meal or a specialized nutrient-dense snack to keep your recovery on track.

Is the platform suitable for injury recovery?

Yes, we have specialized protocols like the 'Joint & Tendon Shield' that focus on collagen synthesis, micronutrient timing, and anti-inflammatory diets specifically formulated for structural tissue repair.
